# Meaning Translation and Confirmation Contract

## Purpose

This contract prevents the system from treating a memorable phrase, source trace or framework label as shared understanding. It creates one reusable **Meaning Record** for every load-bearing conclusion before that conclusion is compressed, inherited or explained to a client.

> **source words and evidence → stated interpretation → observable reality → human confirmation → compressed decision → client explanation → downstream use**

A Meaning Record does not create a belief, market fact, strategy or claim. It makes clear what an authorised source means, what is still an interpretation, and what a later reader must preserve.

## When a Meaning Record is required

Create one for a material conviction, market conclusion, strategic interpretation, commercial route choice, Foundation declaration, Brand decision or other conclusion that later work will rely on. A provisional M00 observation may create a `meaning-hypothesis`; it cannot create a confirmed Meaning Record.

One record may support several documents only while its source, scope, decision and confirmation remain current. Do not bundle unrelated meanings under a memorable umbrella phrase.

## Required contents

Use the controlled template. Every record contains:

1. **Source and evidence** — exact words, source owner/date, evidence IDs, evidence strength and relevant contradiction.
2. **Interpretation** — proposed meaning, strongest plausible alternative and unresolved ambiguity. Interpretation is never silently promoted to fact.
3. **Concrete grounding** — a recognisable situation, people and action, the decision or behaviour it changes, practical consequence, and the opposite or failure mode it rules out.
4. **Boundary** — what the record does not establish, claim limit, scope and return trigger.
5. **Human confirmation** — authorised person’s exact response. Allowed states: `confirmed`, `revision-requested`, `not-established`, `return-upstream`, or `not-required` only when the record remains explicitly provisional.
6. **Compression and expression** — approved short label, reverse-expansion sentence, plain-language client explanation and repeat-back sentence. Compression follows confirmation; it never substitutes for it.
7. **Lifecycle** — ID, version, source hashes, consumers, stale condition and return owner.

### Material planning-horizon decisions

Where a horizon choice is material to a Commercial Direction, Commercial Cycle or executive decision, use the existing Meaning Record rather than creating a competing planning register. Preserve the decision being planned, approved longer-term context where it exists, selected horizon, rationale, what must be learned, what remains outside the horizon, adaptive freedom, upstream-return condition, next review event and human owner. The record must not invent longer-term ambition or convert a selected cycle duration into a universal method rule.

## Confirmation rule

The operator may offer a careful interpretation but may not invent meaning, use silence as agreement, or infer confirmation because a phrase appears in earlier material. If the client says the words do not mean that, or cannot recognise the concrete situation, revise or return upstream. Resolve the authorised source or decision owner through `source_authority` in Shared Contracts. A founder may confirm their lived conviction where they own that source. Human confirmation does not establish an unobserved external market fact; external evidence retains its source, observation and qualification requirements.

Ask: **“When we say this, do we mean this situation, this choice and this consequence — and not the alternative?”** Record the answer verbatim where it changes a load-bearing decision.

## Module ownership

| Stage | Meaning work it owns | Passage condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M00 | Provisional observations and questions | Remain hypotheses; no downstream authority. |
| M01 | Founder conviction and lived reality | Load-bearing conviction records are confirmed by the authorised conviction source owner, including the founder for their lived convictions. |
| M02 | Evidence-bounded market interpretation | Source, counter-evidence and scope are grounded; untested meaning stays provisional. |
| M03 | Strategic interpretation and choice | The authorised human decision owner confirms the practical choice and trade-off; confirmation does not establish an asserted market fact. |
| M04 / Business Foundation | Commercial meaning and business declaration | Load-bearing Foundation records are included in exact approval. |
| M05 | Brand meaning and expression | A current confirmed record is referenced before semantic promotion or expression. |
| M06–M12 / views | Faithful use | Inherit the record; do not reinterpret it without the owning return. |

## Handoff and client expression

Every material Consultancy Decision Record and semantic inheritance item carries its `meaning-record-id`, version, confirmation state and stale condition. A downstream renderer uses the approved client explanation and repeat-back sentence; it does not translate an internal label ad hoc.

`FW_27` remains the editorial and spoken-clarity gate. This contract supplies the meaning it must express. A readable sentence that changes the confirmed meaning fails; an accurate sentence that a client cannot understand returns to expression, not to silent reinterpretation.

## Fail and return

Do not pass a load-bearing item when its record lacks source words, concrete grounding, applicable confirmation, claim boundary or a clear return path. Mark the affected decision `meaning-unconfirmed`; do not use it as an inherited fixed authority, a client promise, a headline or a production brief. Return to the owning module, not to a downstream writer.
