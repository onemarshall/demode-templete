# Strategy-to-Action Starter Plan Contract

## Governing decision

A relevant accepted strategy produces a recommended, editable **Starter Plan**. The plan explains its implementation consequences as a small set of priorities, initiatives, necessary assets, dependencies, appropriate roles, evidence and review conditions. It does not create another strategy authority. Existing Practitioner Briefs specify the fixed strategic requirements and room for specialist judgement; Starter Plans arrange their implementation into a proposed first sequence. Link them rather than duplicate their authority.

The System already has implementation consequences, Commercial Direction, Commercial Cycle 01 and Working Cycle 01. This contract fills the explicit functional-plan and joined-handoff gap. It adds no numbered module or output collection. The business position is established upstream and recorded in the Foundation; a plan cannot invent or silently alter it.

| Accepted M10 Visual Strategy | `10-visual-starter-plan.md` |

Only relevant, scoped strategies generate a plan. For a non-applicable function record the reason internally and do not create filler. Business Priorities is a derived view of the joined Direction; it is not a new Business Strategy document or another decision gate.

The sequence is: current approved Foundation → relevant accepted strategy and supporting evidence → recommended functional Starter Plan → Commercial Direction selects inherited implementation consequences → accepted Direction → business priorities Starter Plan → M11 cross-functional action decision → human acceptance → M12 integrated operating plan → separately permitted production/execution (specialist concept exploration may precede operating commitments where its own current authority permits) → evidence and review. Plans may be issued after their source strategy is accepted and before the whole strategy pack is finished; cross-functional integration waits for the governing Direction and M11 decisions. A plan alone cannot activate a consequence.

Commercial Cycle 01 remains M11's client-facing prioritised action decision. Working Cycle 01 remains the single integrated first operating plan; do not create a competing `integrated-plan.md`. Commercial Direction supplies the broader direction. Near-term 90 days and a 12-month directional view are useful defaults when justified under FW_24; selected horizons and rationale remain explicit and may differ. Later detail decreases as uncertainty increases.

## Implementation judgement and review

A Starter Plan actively interprets approved strategy. Source consequences identify the work and protected meaning; they need not predefine every campaign type, route or implementation idea. The planner may recommend novel bounded routes, explain why they fit this situation and compare their objective contribution, evidence requirements, prerequisites, limitations and trade-offs. New audience, offer, strategic meaning, channel role or claim still returns upstream. A planning route is neither a finished creative concept nor an activated campaign.

Distinguish alternatives from complementary, enabling and sequential work. Recommend a coherent starting combination and strongest credible alternative; never assume every proposal will run. Use the number of materially different directions the situation warrants, without a fixed campaign count or filler. Each proposed route needs a source-specific rationale and a useful hypothesis, not the same source sentence repeated under headings. The planner may withhold one unsupported route while recommending another.

Present the route/plan set for review and re-evaluation. Ask which recommendation the client questions, why, and whether to refine, compare a new alternative, combine bounded elements, defer or stop. Revise the recommendation before sign-off; client disagreement is input to judgement, not a gate failure to overcome. Maintain versioned predecessors and current source pins. Sign-off records the exact current plan/version, selected routes or combined interpretation, conditions, and what the decision settles. Unselected proposals remain unadopted.

The staged path is: approved strategy → recommended route/plan set → discuss/compare/refine/re-evaluate → exact human plan selection → Campaign Creative Brief and concept exploration → concept selection and blueprint/specialist development → separately authorised assets/execution → evidence/review. A selected plan route settles the implementation direction; it does not settle a finished concept, first-cycle quantities, budget, production or publication. M11/M12 retain their actual commitment/scheduling jobs. Existing os-campaign-concept, os-campaign-blueprint and os-produce-campaign retain their authority checks.

Use one human review surface to record distinct decisions when the relevant exact versions and scopes are presented together. Do not ask the client to approve the same substantive choice twice: a plan-route sign-off may also approve its exact Campaign Creative Brief if that brief is present and the response explicitly covers both. This still records both meanings. Concept exploration needs the current Initiative Authority/route permission and the approved brief under the existing concept skill; do not derive either from plan enthusiasm. Missing authority permits only the allowed analysis/brief-preparation stage. Later concept, blueprint, operating commitment, spend and release decisions are distinct because they settle different work.

After sign-off, retain the accepted version and change only affected descendants. A material change to accepted route meaning, scope, protected decisions, combinations or brief conditions needs renewed selection/affected approvals; a permitted editorial clarification or specialist choice does not force blanket reapproval. New evidence, capacity or professional judgement may reopen the plan. Re-evaluate rather than silently continuing stale creative work.

## Required opening and eleven sections

Every plan uses normal FW_23 metadata, a four-row Document details definition list (Role, Prepared for, Version, Status), its Boundary quote and then this exact statement:

> This is the recommended starter plan derived from the strategy agreed through the Langfell process. It provides an initial route into implementation. It may be reviewed, expanded or modified by the people responsible for execution as further operational knowledge, evidence and specialist judgement become available.

Cover the following content areas with reader-led headings (the bold names identify content, not mandatory visible labels): **Strategic objective; Strategic basis; Priorities; Required initiatives; Campaign requirements; Required assets; Dependencies; Ownership; Timing; Evidence and measurement; Review conditions**. A section may say not required, withheld, blocked or open with its source-specific reason; it cannot silently disappear. Add a compact Specialist handoff covering the decision, why, required outcome, fixed choices, open specialist judgement, dependencies and reconsideration conditions. Do not repeat the entire strategy.

Campaigns are conditional implementation proposals. They may be newly recommended from the approved objective and consequences without a prewritten campaign requirement or name. Where supported, record purpose, approved objective/audience/proposition, intended outcome as a hypothesis, approximate timing, dependencies and evidence requirements. Do not invent an unsupported campaign premise, new strategic channel role, finished creative concept, media commitment, budget or publication authority. A campaign requirement is not a locked campaign concept or blueprint. Invoke existing campaign and asset routes only after their own authorities are present.

## Ownership and implementation freedom

Source modules own decisions, implementation consequences, Cost Drivers and strategic measurement intent under FW_32. A Starter Plan may recommend a bounded implementation approach derived from those consequences, make dependencies explicit and propose a relative sequence. This planning freedom is distinct from a view renderer copying a fixed strategy. Its proposals remain recommendations; they cannot establish a new buyer, offer, channel role, claim, strategic priority, production scope or monetary estimate. Mark missing evidence or operational knowledge rather than manufacture certainty.

Commercial Direction selects the portfolio and broad sequence. M11 reconciles cross-functional priorities and conflicts only within its selected Active / First Phase scope; it sets exact quantities, capacity assumptions, ownership commitments, operational definitions and thresholds. A strategic portfolio change returns to Direction or the owning module. M12 schedules and tracks accepted choices, preserving exact M11 commitments. Specialists may develop tactics, execution, creative treatment, technology, processes and permitted sequencing inside the strategy and delegated decision rights; a protected strategy change returns upstream.

Recommended owners are functions or roles supported by the work. An unassigned role stays unassigned; never invent named people, capacity, budgets, spend, targets, calendar dates or resource availability. Unknown capacity prevents a claim that concurrent work is feasible. A plan may expose the question and recommend a conditional order. Applicable commercial-cycle buyer, measurement and response fields remain required; an enabling/non-commercial work item may use an explicitly justified not-applicable field approved in M11 rather than a fabricated metric or buyer activity.

## Two separate status axes

| Recommendation class | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Required | Necessary consequence of an accepted decision; not permission to execute |
| Recommended | Current best bounded implementation recommendation |
| Optional | Useful within the strategy but not essential |
| Open | Requires leadership or specialist judgement |
| Blocked | A named dependency or decision prevents proceeding |
| Withheld | Evidence is insufficient for a responsible recommendation; state the gap and owner |

Human lifecycle is separately **Proposed, Recommended, Approved, Amended, Rejected, Implemented, Under review**. A Recommended class is not an Approved lifecycle. Required is never automatically adopted. Approved needs an exact human decision event for the current action version; Amended/Under review requires the recorded human change/review and is not assumed approved for execution. Implemented requires both prior human adoption and actual completion evidence. Preserve rejected and withheld records; do not hide them to make the pack look complete.

Store machine lifecycle separately from FW_23 document, client-decision, evidence, source-authority and production states. Planning adoption does not approve production, publication, budget, spend or stronger claims. A blocker is lifted only by a new current record and its governing decision, not by relabelling the old item.

## Provenance and machine record

Use `templates/strategy-to-action/plan.schema.json` version 1.0 for the planning transport. Keep source snapshots, exact IDs, versions/hashes, decision/consequence/evidence references, plan/action IDs, class and lifecycle, dependencies, assets, role proposals, uncertainty, adoption events, review and invalidation under `RUN_ROOT/strategy-to-action/`. Readable files retain normal FW_23 metadata and plain explanations; do not print machine control ledgers in client prose. A compact source title/reference is useful where it explains a practical action.

The transport is an adapter to existing FW_32 consequence and measurement records, not another business or approval authority. Map actual canonical records; do not manufacture approved source metadata. The exported source approval event must bind the exact canonical source hash independently of the adapter snapshot hash; repinning a snapshot cannot renew approval.

Default period labels are relative proposals, not mandatory client calendars. A current accepted Direction/M11 source may supply a different ordered planning-period map through `period_source`. Quantities, dates or monetary facts may be quoted only from an exact current accepted field reference (`numeric_refs`); this preserves legitimate source-backed operational knowledge without letting a plan create new commitments or spend authority. Use `context` to quote accepted values without creating a new commitment. A `commitment` quotation from M11 must match the same action and its accepted current period; it cannot silently become a later commitment. The validator’s field checks are not semantic proof of every number in prose.

Supporting evidence can be absent, qualified or contradicted; retain its state. An accepted decision may require implementation without empirical demand proof, and an explicitly authorised evidence-test consequence may recommend bounded learning while preserving its hypothesis. Map that source-owned evidence logic explicitly; do not upgrade it to observed evidence or make proven demand an extra entry prerequisite. 

A consequential recommendation without adequate support is Open, Blocked or Withheld. Each major action must resolve to a current source decision and implementation consequence; a generic industry checklist fails even when headings are present.

## M11 cross-functional resolution and M12 integration

M11 inventories every relevant plan and reconciles duplicates, shared assets, dependencies, cycles, competing priorities, ownership and capacity conflicts. Record each material proposal's selected, deferred, excluded, evidence-needed or upstream-return disposition and reason. Do not move the whole proposed backlog into the active cycle. Resolve or explicitly block conflicts before acceptance; unknown capacity is a decision gap. Each selected action preserves its source IDs and passes existing Initiative Authority, route, claims, investment and FW_32 gates.

Carry the exact selection/dispositions, adoption events, role commitments, capacities, quantities, measures, review and permission boundaries in existing `11_09`/accountability transport. M12 joins these with the Starter Plan references into existing `12_00`–`12_08`: shared assets in `12_01`, sequenced cross-functional work and dependency conditions in `12_03`, evidence in `12_05`, return conditions in `12_06` and cycle transition in `12_08`. Preserve response/rhythm/checklist fields for applicable commercial work. Known dependency and resource conflicts cannot be declared resolved merely because a schedule was emitted.

The initial operating view covers immediate prerequisites, days 1–30, 31–60 and 61–90 when that cycle is selected, with ownership, dependencies, enabling assets, evidence and review. The later-horizon view, including months 4–12 only when that horizon is selected, inherits Direction's broad phases and progression conditions; it adds no detailed dates, budgets, quantities or committed activity beyond their source authority. Link the existing specialist briefs and new plans so implementation can begin without reconstructing strategy.

## Invalidation, refusal and checks

A material source change, withdrawn approval, changed scope/claims/route, rejected or amended adoption, unmet dependency, capacity contradiction or failed assumption invalidates affected recommendations and schedules. Halt affected work, name the source owner, approve an upstream change where required, regenerate affected plans and reconsider adoption. Unaffected records and historical evidence remain intact; no silent overwrite or automatic reapproval.

Before recommended status check authority, exact source hashes, scope, eleven sections/opening, derivation, evidence qualification, both axes, conditional campaigns, specialist freedom and complete output set. Before integration check accepted Direction/M11, all dispositions, human events, shared dependencies, acyclic sequence, resource feasibility or explicit blocks, inherited commitments and decreasing horizon precision. Record first failures and repairs. Static checks are not semantic quality, human comprehension, specialist usefulness, module completion or release approval.

`dev/render-starter-plans.py` validates and renders a source-mapped planning transport after practitioner/AI derivation. It is not an autonomous strategy interpreter, approval service or complete M11/M12 runner. `dev/test-strategy-to-action.py` exercises authority, adoption, conflict, blocked/withheld, invalidation and horizon controls with isolated fictional inputs and actual Markdown renders. Full live delivery and independent specialist/reader validation remain separate evidence requirements.

## Local review transport

The existing planning adapter accepts optional implementation-direction rationale and typed route relationships. A versioned review may retain the predecessor hash and record exact human route selection bound to the plan and current source pins. This is a planning decision only; action lifecycle/adoption and M11 permissions are unchanged. The bounded local selector rejects conflicting alternatives: to combine them, first write and review a coherent revised route rather than silently activating both. Its next-stage field identifies creative-brief review only. Existing Initiative Authority and exact Creative Brief approval settle concept permission; the adapter neither supplies nor validates that permission. No parallel authority envelope is added.

## Concrete plans a reader can use

Apply FW_23/FW_27 before rendering. Open with what this particular plan is for and the recommended starting work. Preserve the mandatory introduction, then orient the reader before listing method or controls. A reader must be able to picture what someone will do and what useful result should exist.

For each task or candidate campaign, write a concrete action heading and connected explanation: what to do or explore; why it serves this strategy and audience; what the work involves and produces; the meaningful trade-off; what must happen first; who should take it forward; and what remains for specialist judgement. Show the relationship to the other choices in ordinary language. A campaign direction is an intelligible proposal with an audience, intended outcome, reason to choose or reject it, next step and limits; it is not a finished concept or an automatic commitment. Use the amount of detail needed to act or review without inventing resources, outcomes, counts or creative decisions.

Keep internal IDs, source maps, status axes and control terms in private records. Translate meaning rather than displaying fields such as `objective_contribution`, `hypothesis` or `relationships`. Prefer familiar objects and verbs: prepare a sample report; check that it accurately represents the service; ask permission before showing a real customer's case. Do not merely replace jargon with shorter vague words, erase rationale or bury the recommendation beneath caution. Place a necessary qualification beside the work it limits.

Eleven required content areas remain covered, but their visible headings describe the reader's question or work; they are not a dump of internal field names. Retain usable dependencies, trade-offs, source qualifications and specialist latitude. Selected, alternative, deferred and blocked directions must be immediately distinguishable without knowing the System.

Before issue, a named authoring reviewer reads the actual rendered sentences and sections against the source: can the reader understand the plan's purpose, recommended route, concrete task/result, reason, sequence, responsibility and open choices? Locate weak passages and revise them. Headings, filled fields or a jargon scan alone do not establish implementability or comprehension. Record this content review separately from structural controls; independent first-read and practitioner checks stay pending until actually performed.

## Specialist planning responsibility

For scoped Marketing, Sales, Content and Proof, Digital and Visual Starter Plans use the corresponding project planner: `os-marketing-planner`, `os-sales-planner`, `os-content-planner`, `os-digital-planner`, `os-visual-planner`. Their shared method is `.agents/skills/os-implementation-planning/SKILL.md` at the workspace root. `os-plan-coordinator` selects the relevant roles and reconciles actual recommendations, dependencies, shared work, competing choices and unresolved resources. Use only applicable accepted strategies, rather than commissioning all roles or inventing missing strategy. Brand/Business outputs retain their existing owners.

The roles contribute domain judgement and return read-only recommendations to the operator; the operator persists scoped candidates and checks sources, prose and release boundaries. They do not become new strategy/approval authorities or replace existing campaign/production skills. Coordinate within configured capacity; no concurrency or model settings are changed. A newly created role must be available in the session catalog before claiming its use. Catalog loading/restart and configuration checks are separate from proof that specialist judgement improves real plans.
