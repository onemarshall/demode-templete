# Brand Semantic Derivation and Promotion Contract

## Purpose

This contract prevents an inherited phrase from gaining Brand authority merely because it is available, memorable, repeated or traceable. It governs the semantic reasoning between an approved Business Foundation and Module 05.

The mandatory sequence is:

> **source authority → semantic meaning → strategic level → strategic judgement → Brand decision → expression role → contextual use**

Skipping a stage is a hard failure. Compatibility with an upstream source is not inheritance from that source.

## Governing objects

### Semantic Inheritance Ledger

Before any Module 05 field is drafted, generate one internal ledger from the exact approved Foundation and authorised M01–M04, route, commercial and claims sources. It is not Brand copy and has no public-expression authority. It records each material item’s exact language, semantic role, authority, preservation and translation permissions, promotion permissions, public-copy state, boundaries, evidence and invalidation condition.

The ledger must support at least: `governing-belief`, `conviction`, `broken-thing`, `hidden-truth`, `governing-stand`, `purpose-source`, `vision-source`, `customer-problem`, `customer-recognition`, `customer-belief-shift`, `strategic-position`, `mechanism`, `operating-commitment`, `distinction`, `proof-commitment`, `evidence-boundary`, `claims-boundary`, `governing-declaration`, `diagnostic-expression`, `mechanism-memory`, `natural-language-candidate`, `narrative-expression`, `commercial-fact`, `audience-decision` and `anti-audience-decision`.

`strategic-position` records the source-owned business position: audience, offer/work, customer value, commitments, governing belief/stand and constraints. Module 05 develops brand positioning from that approved position through meaning, identity and expression. It cannot silently change the business position; a necessary change returns to the relevant M01–M04 or named fact, offer, economics or evidence owner. This clarifies the existing ledger type and creates no new field or deliverable.

Every material item also references its current `FW_35` Meaning Record. Brand work may select expression, but it may not substitute an elegant interpretation for the confirmed concrete meaning.

### Recognition continuity

For every material Foundation diagnosis, buyer situation or source-owner-visible burden, the ledger must record its **recognition cue**: the concrete condition a right reader should be able to recognise in their own life or business. Module 05 must preserve that cue or propose a clearer practical expression of it. Authoring review may assess likely recognition; it cannot establish actual recognition or comprehension without appropriate reception evidence. It may change the wording, order, medium or level of detail; it may not quietly make the condition more abstract, narrower, weaker or less recognisable.

If a proposed Brand expression changes, combines or omits a material recognition cue, record the source wording, the proposed expression, the recognition gained and lost, the reason, the strongest alternative and `founder-decision-needed: yes`. The candidate must present that judgement as a Brand decision to the authorised human decision owner; it cannot treat it as routine copy editing. `founder-decision-needed` remains the compatibility field name. It identifies a required authorised human decision, including a founder only where they hold that source or decision authority.

Any item intended to carry a load-bearing Brand role must be classified. If an exact inherited field is labelled Core Belief, Governing Declaration, Purpose, buyer, current offer, claims boundary or another fixed authority, its value must match the owning source. A derived or compatible phrase must carry its derived role instead.

### Brand hierarchy

Build the following hierarchy before filling the M05 schema:

1. governing identity;
2. broken condition;
3. governing stand;
4. purpose;
5. vision;
6. customer reality;
7. desired belief movement;
8. strategic position;
9. mechanism;
10. operating commitments;
11. distinction;
12. proof and evidence;
13. boundary; and
14. expression system.

The hierarchy is an internal reasoning object, not a second strategy authority. It must name the strongest collapse point: the governing idea whose removal causes the greatest loss of Brand meaning.

### Decision and expression records

Every load-bearing choice records:

- the strategic decision: what the Brand must represent;
- the expression decision: which wording, image, structure or device serves a named reader and context;
- `decision-confidence: high | medium | low`;
- reason, evidence strength and alternative strength; and
- `founder-decision-needed: yes | no`.

An expression does not become the strategy because it is elegant.

## Promotion Gate

Run the gate whenever an item moves to a higher strategic level or more authoritative placement. This includes candidate language becoming a signature, a mechanism becoming purpose, an operating commitment becoming primary distinction, a declaration becoming a headline, a belief shift becoming a tagline, or a signature becoming a presentation opening.

Record:

- original source and role;
- proposed role;
- why the move is needed;
- represented and omitted meaning;
- strongest alternatives;
- reader job and contextual suitability;
- evidence or strategic basis;
- downside of choosing and not choosing;
- whether the move changes the hierarchy; and
- final recommendation.

Repetition, memorability, brevity, inclusion in a language bank, downstream use or anchor trace never passes the gate by itself.

## Comparative judgement

For a materially open direction, generate meaningfully different candidates. For primary Brand expression, consider governing-belief, governing-stand, customer-recognition, purpose, invitation, distinction, mechanism and vision families where relevant. Compare Brand coverage, belief and stand, purpose, buyer recognition, comprehension, distinction, memorability, behavioural ownability, voice, evidence, burden, resonance, contextual use, oversimplification and category or software confusion.

Do not use mechanical scoring as the decision. Recommend one route, state why it wins and what it gives up, and retain up to two materially different alternatives as decision evidence only. Alternatives do not gain downstream authority.

## Signature decision

The Brand-signature record must use one of: `selected`, `selected-with-qualification`, `candidate`, `deferred`, `not-required`.

`deferred` is a complete strategic decision when the Brand is clear but no permanent line has earned the role. Role-specific language may still be selected for recognition, stand, diagnosis, mechanism, invitation or boundary. A signature is never required to make M05 complete.

## Purpose, vision, values and distinction

- Purpose answers why the business exists, survives a mechanism rename and connects broken condition to action and intended future.
- Vision describes the intended future and preserves its evidence status; it is not a mechanism or performance claim.
- Values name chosen behaviour and accepted cost. Operating disciplines, method rules and evidence rules remain separately typed even when related.
- Distinction represents the whole meaningful operating difference. A subordinate mechanism may receive emphasis only when comparative judgement shows why.

## Presentation strategy

Before slides, record audience, prior knowledge, supported decision, first necessary understanding, central Brand idea, misunderstanding to prevent, intended memory, included and excluded decisions, narrative sequence and its rationale.

Then make a separate Presentation Opening decision. Its job is to orient the viewer, identify the Brand Strategy, make the central tension or belief meaningful and create a reason to continue. `primary-signature-role` and `presentation-opening-role` are independent. Assess the first two slides together against the cold-viewer questions. An authoring or AI assessment predicts reception; only an appropriately uncoached human trial can supply cold-viewer evidence.

## Adversarial and comprehension tests

Every M05 candidate runs removal, substitution, governing-idea, founder-explanation, stranger, promotion, presentation, specialist and anti-fragmentation tests. Before the authorised human decision, test the joined Brand Strategy, Strategy Summary, presentation opening and practitioner handoff for what a reader thinks the business is, the problem, stand, audience, distinction and promise. Record misunderstanding against the owning decision.

Structural, AI or same-operator review cannot be labelled a cold-human pass. Record the actual review method and its limits; predicted recognition, comprehension or distinctiveness is not a demonstrated reception result.

## Authority, manifest and invalidation

The Canonical Strategy remains the only M05 strategy authority. The ledger, hierarchy, promotion records, alternatives, Presentation Strategy and test working are reasoning controls. Derived views may only inherit the selected recommendation.

`RUN-MANIFEST.md` may report exact source hashes, ledger ID/hash, decision IDs, states, selected route, output hashes and stale conditions. Its labels do not create, rename or enlarge semantic authority.

The ledger becomes stale when its exact Foundation, any consumed M01–M04 authority, RouteAuthorization, commercial conclusion, claim boundary or evidence state changes. A stale ledger closes M05 derivation and every dependent candidate enters review. Historical runs are never silently rewritten.

## Genericity test

The contract must pass a fixture whose beliefs, market, audience, offer, language and mechanism are unrelated to Langfell. Client-specific phrases may appear only in client runs, never in this contract or its reusable tests as the expected universal answer.
