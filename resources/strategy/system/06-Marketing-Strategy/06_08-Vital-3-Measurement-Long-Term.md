# Deliverable 06: Vital 3 Measurement & Long-Term

**Langfell™ Belief Shift Strategy**
**Phase 4: Execution & Measurement**

## Identity & Purpose

Set up measurement for statement-based marketing — including derivative coherence tracking.

```text
### Vital 3 Metrics (Statement-Based)

| Metric               | Definition                                     | Target   | Method               |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------- | -------------------- |
| **Clarity**          | % of team who can articulate all 10 statements | [Target] | Quarterly quiz       |
| **Proof Match Test** | % of content derived from statements           | [Target] | Content audit        |
| **Result**           | Three separable outcomes — measure each (see below) | [Target] | See outcome table |

### Result — Outcome Decomposition

"Result" is not one number. Belief Shift Strategy produces three separable outcomes, each measured on its own and capable of failing independently:

| Outcome                | What It Measures                                                                  | Signal                                                  | Method                          |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Cognitive adoption** | Audience now holds the belief (FROM → TO has actually moved)                      | Customers use statement language unprompted             | Testimonial / interview analysis |
| **Purchase behaviour** | Audience buys                                                                     | Conversion rate, revenue, sales-cycle length            | Sales / CRM data                |
| **Advocacy**           | Audience identifies with the belief publicly and equips others to hold it        | Referrals, shares, user-generated proof, NPS detractors of competitors | Referral tracking / social listening |

A campaign can produce cognitive adoption without purchase (right belief, wrong moment), purchase without advocacy (transactional fit only), or advocacy without purchase (the belief alone aligns them). When results lag, name which of the three is missing before adjusting the statements.

---

### Derivative Coherence Tracking

**Question:** Is all content derived from the 10 statements?

| Content Type  | Statement Source   | Coherent? |
| ------------- | ------------------ | --------- |
| Ads           | Statements 1, 3    | " / --    |
| Landing pages | Statements 3, 4, 6 | " / --    |
| Emails        | Statements 1-6     | " / --    |
| Social posts  | Various            | " / --    |

**Monthly Coherence Audit:**

- Pull sample of recent content
- Trace each piece to source statement
- Flag content not derived from statements

---

### Statement Drift Monitoring

| Question                          | Pass | Fail | Action            |
| --------------------------------- | ---- | ---- | ----------------- |
| Are all 10 statements being used? | Yes  | No   | Review deployment |
| Is content traced to statements?  | Yes  | No   | Content audit     |
| Can team articulate statements?   | Yes  | No   | Retraining        |
| Are customers echoing language?   | Yes  | No   | Adjust statements |

---

### Long-Term Statement Evolution

| Horizon | Focus     | Statement Evolution                                      |
| ------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Year 1  | Establish | Deploy all 10, refine based on response                  |
| Year 2  | Optimise  | A/B test variations, strengthen winners                  |
| Year 3+ | Evolve    | Update for market changes (return to Module 05 if major) |

**Statement Update Protocol:**

- Minor refinements: Update within Module 06
- Major changes: Return to Module 05, re-derive

---

### What We'll Say No To

| Opportunity                 | Why Decline            | Protects             |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------- |
| Content not from statements | Breaks coherence       | Derivative coherence |
| Off-brand messaging         | Contradicts statements | Brand integrity      |
| Volume over fit             | Dilutes statements     | Statement impact     |
```

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Vital 3 includes statement-specific measures
- [ ] Derivative coherence tracking established
- [ ] Statement drift monitoring scheduled
- [ ] Long-term evolution protocol defined

---

**If stuck:** If you don't know what to measure, start with one signal per outcome. Cognitive adoption: are customers using your belief language unprompted? Purchase: are conversion rates moving for belief-led traffic versus feature-led? Advocacy: are customers referring without being asked? One signal per outcome beats five signals on one.
