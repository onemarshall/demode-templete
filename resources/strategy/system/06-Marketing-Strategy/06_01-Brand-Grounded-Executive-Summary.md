# Deliverable 01: Brand-Grounded Executive Summary

**Langfell™ Belief Shift Strategy**
**Phase 1: Foundation & Brand Alignment**
**v4.2 | Statement-Based Architecture**

---

## Identity & Purpose

Write a strategic overview that leads with brand and previews the 10-statement output structure.

---

## Inputs Required (from Module 05)

```text
- Brand Strategy Statement
- Brand Beliefs (World, Customer, Brand)
- Belief Shift (FROM → TO)
- X-Factor
- Filter (Who we're for / not for)
- Tagline
```

---

## Output Template

```text
## Brand-Grounded Executive Summary

---

### Brand Foundation

**Brand Strategy:**

> "[From Module 05]"

**Why This Matters Now:** [Context]

**What Success Looks Like:** [Brand becomes standard]

---

### Target Audience

| Segment            | Description   | Priority  |
| ------------------ | ------------- | --------- |
| **Belief-Ready**   | [From Filter] | Primary   |
| **Belief-Curious** | [Description] | Secondary |
| **Not For Us**     | [From Filter] | Excluded  |

---

### Statement-Based Output Preview

This module will produce 10 Marketing Positioning Statements:

**Belief Journey (Statements 1-7):**
| # | Statement | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Problem Awareness | Names the unseen problem |
| 2 | Belief Challenge | Challenges limiting belief |
| 3 | New Truth | Introduces empowering belief |
| 4 | Proof | Demonstrates evidence |
| 5 | Differentiation | Why us, not them |
| 6 | Invitation | First engagement |
| 7 | Advocacy | Equips sharing |

**Positioning (Statements 8-10):**
| # | Statement | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | Positioning | Full positioning statement |
| 9 | X-Factor | Competitor-proof differentiator |
| 10 | Value | Worth beyond price |

---

### Success Metrics

| Category             | Metric                                | Target   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Traditional          | [Metrics]                             | [Target] |
| **Clarity**          | Team can articulate all 10 statements | [Target] |
| **Proof Match Test** | All content derives from statements   | [Target] |
| **Result**           | Customers echo statement language     | [Target] |
```

---

## Quality Gate

- [ ] Brand Strategy expressed
- [ ] 10-statement structure previewed
- [ ] Audience aligned with Filter
- [ ] Vital 3 metrics established

---

**If stuck:** The executive summary should read like a sharp answer to "What is this brand and why does it matter?" If it reads like a Wikipedia article, you're describing instead of positioning. Go back to your Brand Strategy and pull out the single sharpest thing about this brand — that's your lead.
