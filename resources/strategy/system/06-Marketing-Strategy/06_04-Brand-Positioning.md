# Deliverable 04: Brand Positioning Statements 8-10

**Langfell™ Belief Shift Strategy**
**Phase 2: Audience & Brand Positioning**
**v4.2 | Statement-Based Architecture**

---

## Identity & Purpose

Write the three positioning statements (8-10) as specific marketing language.

**Critical:** Outputs are actual statements, not placeholders.

---

```text

- Claims Boundaries
```

## Quality Gate

**Pass Criteria:**

- [ ] Statement 8 is WRITTEN (not "For [audience]..." placeholder)
- [ ] Statement 9 is WRITTEN (not "[X-Factor]" placeholder)
- [ ] Statement 10 is WRITTEN (not "[Value]" placeholder)
- [ ] Each statement is 1-3 sentences
- [ ] Each statement is SPECIFIC to this business
- [ ] Each statement follows Copywriting Framework
- [ ] Each statement is within Claims Boundaries

**Fail Criteria:**

- Any statement contains brackets like "[audience]" or "[benefit]"
- Statements could apply to any business
- Statements don't follow brand voice

**If FAIL:** Rewrite until all three are actual written statements.

---

## Examples

**Bad (Placeholder):**

> "For [Belief-Ready audience] who [problem], [Brand] is the [category] that [benefit]."

**Good (Written):**

> "For strategy-paralysed builders who've been told they need months of market research before they can act, Langfell is the conviction-based strategy framework that transforms your beliefs into validated business strategy in weeks, not months. We don't ask 'what does the market want?' we ask 'what do you believe?'"

**Bad (Generic):**

> "We're different because we're better and care more about our customers."

**Good (Specific):**

> "While traditional consultants charge GBP 10,000+ to tell you what the market wants, we charge under GBP 400 to help you prove what you already believe. We're not researchers. We're belief validators."

---

**If stuck:** A positioning statement is specific enough that a competitor can't use it. If yours works for three other brands in your space, it's not positioning — it's describing a category. Push deeper into what your conviction makes uniquely yours.
