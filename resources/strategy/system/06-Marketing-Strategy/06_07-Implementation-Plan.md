# 06_07 Activation Requirements

This output tells Module 11 what marketing must have available. It does not create a marketing calendar.

## Required inputs

- Module 04 Response Path and RouteAuthorization
- Module 05 Brand Strategy, Filter and Claims Boundaries
- the ten locked marketing statements
- the Module 06 channel roles

## Output shape

```text
## 06_07 Activation Requirements

### Required Marketing Assets
| Requirement | Statements used | Channel job | Dependency | Readiness test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [asset/capability] | [statement numbers] | [role] | [source/approval] | [condition] |

### Founder or Team Readiness
- [what must be understood or practised]

### Evidence and Approval Requirements
- [buyer language to capture]
- [proof or claims review]
- [human approval]

### Module 11 Handoff
- priority requirements Module 11 may select
- constraints and unavailable assets
- no quantities, cadence, dates, targets or thresholds
```

## Gate

Fail this output if it contains a week, quarter, publishing frequency, outreach volume, campaign budget, numerical target or success threshold. Those decisions belong to Module 11; Module 12 schedules them.
