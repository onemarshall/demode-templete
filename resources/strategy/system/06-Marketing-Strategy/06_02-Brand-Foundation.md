# Deliverable 02: Brand Foundation

**Langfell™ Belief Shift Strategy**
**Phase 1: Foundation & Brand Alignment**
**v4.2 | Statement-Based Architecture**

---

## Identity & Purpose

Build the brand-grounded foundation: Brand-Lens SWOT, Competitor Friction Map, and Market Belief-Readiness analysis.

---

## Inputs Required (from Module 05)

```text
- Brand Strategy Statement
- Belief Shift (FROM → TO)
- X-Factor
- Claims Boundaries
- Filter
```

---

## Output Template

```text
## Brand Foundation

---

### Brand-Lens SWOT

| Category    | Item   | Brand Result Impact        |
| ----------- | ------ | -------------------------- |
| Strength    | [Item] | [How supports result]      |
| Weakness    | [Item] | [Mitigation]               |
| Opportunity | [Item] | [Belief-ready underserved] |
| Threat      | [Item] | [Response]                 |

---

### Competitor Friction Map

| Competitor | Their Belief | To Copy Our Statements They'd Need To... | Friction |
| ---------- | ------------ | ---------------------------------------- | -------- |
| [Name]     | "[Belief]"   | [SPECIFIC change]                        | H/M/L    |

---

### Belief-Readiness Market Map

| Segment        | %   | Where      | Current Belief  |
| -------------- | --- | ---------- | --------------- |
| Belief-Ready   | [%] | [Channels] | "[FROM]"        |
| Belief-Curious | [%] | [Channels] | "[Questioning]" |
| Not For Us     | [%] | [Channels] | "[Defended]"    |

---

### Statement Implications

| Finding              | Impacts Statements | How                             |
| -------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| [SWOT finding]       | [1, 3, 5...]       | [Adjust language for...]        |
| [Competitor finding] | [5, 9]             | [Strengthen differentiation...] |
```

---

## Quality Gate

- [ ] SWOT through brand lens
- [ ] Competitor Friction specific
- [ ] Belief-readiness mapped
- [ ] Statement implications noted

---

**If stuck:** If the brand foundation reads like cut-and-paste from Module 05, you're copying, not translating. The foundation here should reframe brand elements for marketing use. Ask: "How would I explain this brand to someone who's never heard of us and has 30 seconds of attention?"
