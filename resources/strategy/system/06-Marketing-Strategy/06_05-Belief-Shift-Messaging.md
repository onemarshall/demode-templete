# Deliverable 05: Belief Shift Messaging Statements 1-7

**Langfell™ Belief Shift Strategy**
**Phase 3: Messaging & Channels**
**v4.2 | Statement-Based Architecture**

## Identity & Purpose

**Critical:** This deliverable produces actual written statements — not placeholders like "[Copy]" or "[Message]". Each one is immediately usable.

## The Seven Statements

Each statement is the right input for an audience member **at the named belief stage**. The stages aren't a journey every buyer walks through — audiences enter at different points, and the same campaign may run Statement 1 on one channel and Statement 4 on another. Match statement to stage, not to step number.

| #   | Statement         | Purpose                          | Belief Stage Served          |
| --- | ----------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| 1   | Problem Awareness | Names the problem they don't see | Unaware Problem-Aware        |
| 2   | Belief Challenge  | Challenges limiting belief       | Problem-Aware Belief-Curious |
| 3   | New Truth         | Introduces empowering belief     | Belief-Curious Belief-Ready  |
| 4   | Proof             | Demonstrates evidence            | Belief-Ready Converted       |
| 5†  | Differentiation   | Why us, not them                 | All stages                   |
| 6   | Invitation        | First engagement (not hard sell) | Belief-Ready Converted       |
| 7   | Advocacy          | Equips customers to share        | Converted Advocate           |

† Statement 5 is cross-cutting — it travels alongside whichever other statement the audience first encounters. It is not a stage-sequenced step.

## Statement 6: Invitation Statement

**Stage:** Belief-Ready Converted
**Purpose:** Invite first engagement NOT hard sell

**Written Statement:**

> "[WRITE THE ACTUAL STATEMENT HERE]
>

**How it moves them:** From "I'm interested" to "I'll take the first step"

**Use in:**

- Lead magnet offers
- First-touch invitations
- "Start here" messaging

**How it moves them:** From satisfied customer to active advocate

## Quality Gate

**Pass Criteria (ALL must be met):**

- [ ] All 7 statements are WRITTEN (no "[Copy]" or "[Message]" placeholders)
- [ ] Each statement is 1-3 sentences (focused)
- [ ] Each statement is SPECIFIC to this business (not generic)
- [ ] Language follows Copywriting Framework throughout
- [ ] Belief Shift (FROM → TO) is visible across Statements 1-4
- [ ] Statement 4 (Proof) is within Claims Boundaries
- [ ] Statement 5 (Differentiation) is specific (competitors couldn't say it)
- [ ] Statement 6 (Invitation) is engagement-focused (not hard sell)
- [ ] Statement 7 (Advocacy) provides actual words to share (not just "tell friends")

**Fail Criteria (ANY = fail):**

- Any statement contains placeholders like "[Copy]", "[Message]", "[Insert here]"
- Statements could apply to any brand in the industry
- Belief shift is unclear or missing
- Copywriting Framework is not followed
- Claims in Statement 4 exceed Claims Boundaries

**If FAIL:** Do not proceed. Rewrite until all 7 are actual, specific, written statements.

## Examples

### Bad Statement (Placeholder)

```text
> "[Copy following Copywriting Framework that names the problem]"
```
### Bad Statement (Generic)

```text
> "Many businesses struggle with strategy. They waste time and money on approaches that don't work."
```
### Good Statement (Written, Specific)

```text
> "You've been told you need extensive market research before you can move. Months of surveys. Focus groups. Competitive analysis. Meanwhile, your conviction sits waiting, gathering dust while you gather data. But here's what no one tells you: the builders who win aren't the ones with the best research. They're the ones who knew what they believed and found the fastest path to prove it."
```

---

**If stuck:** The Belief Shift Journey is the engine of this module. If your seven statements don't read as a natural progression from "I didn't know I had this problem" to "I'm telling my friends about this," the journey has gaps. Map each statement to a specific moment of realisation your customer goes through.
