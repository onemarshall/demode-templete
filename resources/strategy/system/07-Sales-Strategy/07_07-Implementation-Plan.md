# 07_07 Sales Activation Requirements

This output tells Module 11 what must be ready before sales activity begins. It does not set outreach volume, call cadence or conversion targets.

## Output shape

```text
## 07_07 Sales Activation Requirements

### Founder or Team Readiness
- understands the eight sales statements
- can run the Five Movements without turning them into a rigid script
- can qualify and disqualify without softening the boundary
- can answer approved objections inside the claims limits

### Required Sales Assets
| Asset | Source statement/movement | Owner | Readiness test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [asset] | [source] | [owner] | [condition] |

### Evidence Capture
- buyer language
- qualification state
- objection and response
- next step and outcome
- claims pressure or overclaim risk

### Module 11 Handoff
- available sales mechanism and assets
- constraints and draft decisions
- no quantities, cadence, dates, targets or thresholds
```

## Gate

The founder or named operator must be able to hold a fit conversation and record what happened. Module 11 later selects and quantifies the route; Module 12 schedules it.
