# Module 03: Business Commitments

**DeMode™**
_Challenging Beliefs to Create Positioning Space_

---

## What This Module Does

Business Commitments turns the belief gaps identified in Module 02 into a business position. You choose which market assumptions to challenge, design the Conviction Grid, define the Belief Shift Journey (FROM → TO), and identify a position competitors would find costly to follow.

Module 02 identified where market beliefs may be false. This module decides how to challenge them and what you will do to prove the alternative before marketing the claim. Those decisions connect market understanding to the brand you will build.

**Input:** Belief Gap Analysis and Reconnaissance Log (from Module 02) + Core Belief and all five conviction elements (from Module 01)
**Output:** Strategic positioning with Conviction Grid, deployable proof commitments, draft Belief Shift Journey, competitor friction analysis and a board-readable Strategic Judgement stating the options considered, recommendation, confidence, commercial consequences, reversal conditions, integrity result and professional call
**Time:** 6-10 hours for a Provisional pass; add 4-8 hours plus real response time for Validated.

**Primary reader:** founders and business owners. In the current commercial path, this may be facilitated inside Tier 4 delivery; long term, Tier 1 should still be understandable with AI support. Write and run the module as founder-readable, not consultant-only.

---

## Why This Module Exists

Module 02 revealed where the market operates on potentially false beliefs. This module decides how to challenge those beliefs strategically.

Not every belief gap is worth pursuing. This module helps you:

- choose which boundaries to challenge (Frontier Analysis);
- design moves that follow from your conviction (Conviction Grid);
- define the customer transformation (draft Belief Shift Journey for Module 05);
- identify what competitors would have to change to follow you (Friction Analysis); and
- commit to proof before marketing (Proof Score).

---

## The Belief-First Approach

Traditional positioning asks: "How do we differentiate?" Business Commitments asks: "What belief do we challenge, and how do we prove we're right?"

| Traditional Strategy | Business Commitments |
| -- | -- |
| Eliminate costs | Question factors and challenge the belief that they're necessary |
| Find white space | Find belief gaps and validate they're worth entering |
| Create barriers | Create friction by building coherent belief + behaviour systems |
| Position for advantage | Position on truth you can prove |

## Key Outputs

### Strategic Judgement

The Strategic Judgement gives leadership a decision to make. It compares credible options, recommends one and explains why. It states the confidence in that recommendation, the commercial stakes and what would change the advice.

### 1. Frontier Analysis

Which belief boundary are we challenging, and why? Includes conviction alignment score and evidence available.

### 2. Conviction Grid

| Action | Factor | Belief Challenge | Proof Commitment | Competitor Friction |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| QUESTION | X | What belief we reject | How we'll prove it | What competitors must change |
| REJECT | X | What belief we de-prioritise | How we'll prove it | What competitors must change |
| COMMIT | X | What belief we embrace | How we'll prove it | What competitors must change |
| INTRODUCE | X | What new belief we introduce | How we'll prove it | What competitors must change |

### 3. Meaningful Divergence

Visual comparison of industry standard vs. your positioning, focused on customer-important factors rather than a numeric percentage target.

### 4. Draft Belief Shift Journey (FROM → TO)

The customer transformation your positioning enables. This is a **draft** for Module 05 to finalise and lock:

> FROM "[limiting belief customers currently hold]"
> TO "[empowering belief your brand offers]"

### 5. Belief Proof Commitment

Each material commitment names an artefact that could make it visible: product behaviour, a policy, story, named framework or diagnostic, documented method, workshop, pricing structure, public commitment or another form of evidence. Module 03 defines what must be proven and what could prove it. Later modules design and deploy the final expression.

3+ specific ways you will PROVE the belief (not just state it): product/feature proof, policy/process proof, story/track record proof.

### 6. Competitor Friction Analysis

What systemic changes would competitors need to make to follow you? Includes honest acknowledgement that friction is not impossibility.

### 7. Commitment Anchors (for Module 04 and then Module 05)

QUESTION and REJECT also carry a **Public Specialisation Signal**: a way for the market to observe the refusal without reading the internal strategy. Record strategic lock separately from earned market credibility. Weak early recognition calls for diagnosis of the credibility gap; it does not automatically unlock the Grid.

Locked outputs ready for Module 04 (Viability Check and Route). Module 04 carries the strategy, proof commitments and risk obligations to Module 05 (Brand Identity), which is the runtime authority for Modules 06-10.

## How to Use This Module

### Run Depth

There are three run modes, but only two pass states:

- **Orientation Scan:** a 60-90 minute triage read of Gates 1, 2, 5, and 6. It helps a founder see whether Module 03 is worth the deeper work now. It does **not** produce a Handoff Pack, does **not** authorise Module 04, and does **not** unlock Module 05.
- **Provisional run:** the minimum authorising run. Complete all internal gates and produce a strategy coherent enough to take into Module 04 in hypothesis mode.
- **Validated run:** complete the Provisional run, then pass the three Validation Tests before Module 05 locks brand identity or any expensive downstream commitment.

Provisional is not failure. It is the honest state before outside pressure-testing. Validated is stronger, but it costs more time and access.

Do not create a third "Minimum Viable Module 03" pass state. If the internal gates are not all passed, the output is an Orientation Scan or an incomplete run, not a Provisional strategy. Skipping Gates 3, 4, 7, or 8 creates exactly the kind of confident-looking strategy this module exists to prevent.

Expect iteration. A meaningful portion of first runs will end Provisional, return upstream or reveal that market evidence is not ready. These are valid findings. They expose unsupported belief before Module 05 carries it into brand, sales, marketing, web and visual work. Do not publish a percentage until DeMode has enough live runs to report observed ranges.

### Step 1: Gather Inputs

Verify you have:

- [ ] Completed Module 02 (Belief Gap Analysis)
- [ ] Builder conviction (from Module 01)
- [ ] Commercial Viability Gate signal (from Module 02)
- [ ] Commitment to prove the belief (not just market it)

### Step 2: Execute the Frontier Analysis

Follow the current execution prompt (`03-Prompt.md`). It retains the same five-part analysis:

1. **Phase 1:** Belief-Maintained Landscape (map factors to beliefs)
2. **Phase 2:** Frontier Analysis (choose which boundary to challenge)
3. **Phase 3:** Conviction Grid Core (design moves with proof commitments)
4. **Phase 4:** Divergence Map & Proof Match Test (verify divergence + 3+ proof points)
5. **Phase 5:** Strategy Validation (confirm alignment, friction, and viability)

**Iteration note:** The Conviction Grid is one-shot at the strategic layer. Once locked, it guides downstream decisions without being reopened whenever pressure arises. Proof commitments, wording and market learning still evolve. Revisit the Grid itself only when the signals in `03-Philosophy.md` appear.

### Step 3: Extract Handoff Pack

Before proceeding to Module 04, extract and lock the Commitment Anchors: The Broken Thing, Core Belief, Proof Commitment, Hidden Truth, Legacy Statement, Belief Shift Journey (FROM → TO), and Competitor Friction Summary.

### Step 4: Validate Before Proceeding

Pass the Vital Check before moving to Module 04.

## Vital Check (Threshold Gate)

**All markers must be met before proceeding:**

| Marker | Evidence Required | ✓ |
| -- | -- | --- |
| Conviction Grid Complete | Every move has belief rationale AND proof commitment | |
| Friction Realistic | Competitor friction based on systemic barriers, not wishful thinking | |
| Meaningful Divergence | Divergence concentrates on customer-important factors (per Module 02 evidence) | |
| Evidence Ready | Each proposed claim has matched evidence and provenance, or an explicit provisional capture-and-review plan | |
| Belief Sharp | Positioning clearly opposes a default industry belief | |
| Belief Shift Journey Defined | FROM → TO stated and validated against customer language | |
| Commercial Viability Gate | This frontier path is worth pursuing (from Module 02) | |

**If all met:** Proceed to Module 04 (Viability Check)
**If any missing:** Gather that evidence before proceeding
**If you cannot meet one:** Follow the Upstream Iteration Map in `03-Quality-Gates.md`. Most Module 03 failures trace upstream -- Gates 1-2 failures usually mean returning to Module 01, Gates 3, 4A, and 7 failures usually mean returning to Module 02. Iteration back through earlier modules is the expected path when Module 03 exposes upstream thinness, not a sign of failure.

**Note:** A marker that fails identifies the work needed before you can proceed.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Symptom | Fix |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Grid without proof | "We'll question X" but no commitment to demonstrate it | Add proof commitment to every move |
| Wishful friction | "Competitors can't copy us" | Analyse what they'd actually need to change honestly |
| Contrarianism | Challenging beliefs just to be different | Ensure conviction alignment AND market evidence |
| Skipping Belief Proof | Marketing belief before proving it | Identify 3+ proof points first |
| Vague Belief Shift Journey | "Customers will think differently" | Define specific FROM and TO beliefs |
| Ignoring viability | Pursuing a frontier no one will pay for | Confirm Commercial Viability Gate before deep work |

---

## What This Module Does NOT Do

- **Create impenetrable moats** -- Competitors CAN copy; it's just costly. Friction buys time, not permanence.
- **Guarantee market success** -- Validation still required in later modules
- **Replace product-market fit** -- Belief without delivery fails
- **Work without genuine conviction** -- You can't fake this
