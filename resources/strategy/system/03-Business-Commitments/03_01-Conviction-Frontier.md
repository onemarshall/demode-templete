## MODULE IDENTITY & PURPOSE

**Business Commitments**identifies belief-defined market boundaries and creates differentiated positioning space through conviction + proof + Belief Shift Journey.

**DeMode Distinction:**

- Generic strategy = Factor changes (traditional grid).
- **Business Commitments**= **Belief challenges** + **Proof commitments** + **Belief Shift Journey (FROM TO)** + **Competitor friction analysis**.

**Governing Principle: Belief-First, Proof-Always**

- Belief authorises the strategy.
- Proof cements it in reality.
- Result validates market adoption.

**Flow:** Module 02 (Recon) → **Business Commitments**→ Module 04 (Economics) → Module 05 (Brand Identity).

**Builder Role:** Provide context, validate conviction, commit to proof.
**AI Role:** Execute 5-phase analysis.

PHASE 4: DIVERGENCE MAP, BELIEF SHIFT JOURNEY & BELIEF PROOF ALIGNMENT (20%)

**4A. Divergence Map Analysis:**

| [Factor 1] | [Level] | [Level] | up/down/= | Yes / No / Partial |
| [Factor 2] | [Level] | [Level] | up/down/= | Yes / No / Partial |
...

**Meaningful Divergence Check:**
Of the factors where you diverge from consensus, what proportion are Customer-Important (per Module 02 evidence)?

- If most of your divergence is on customer-important factors: pass.
- If your divergence is spread across low-importance factors: fail -- divergence is mathematically present but practically hollow. Concentrate positioning on factors customers actually care about.
- In regulated or standardised markets, total divergence may be structurally constrained. Document the constraint; concentrate on the few open dimensions that matter.

See `03-Quality-Gates.md` Gate 7 for the full Meaningful Divergence test. Prior iterations used a 70% numeric threshold; that heuristic has been retired in favour of the customer-importance question the threshold was trying to approximate.

## WHEN IT DOESN'T WORK

If the Business Commitments positioning doesn't gain market traction after 90 days:

1. **Check Clarity:** Is the belief sharp enough? (Conviction Clarity Test)
2. **Check Proof Match Test:** Are you actually proving it? (Review 3 proof points)
3. **Check Belief Shift Journey:** Does the FROM TO match actual customer language?
4. **Check Market Fit:** Is the frontier analysis the right one?
5. **If all four are strong:** The belief may not resonate. Return to Phase 2 and reassess the Frontier Analysis selection.

**Recovery Path:**

- Revisit Module 02 Belief Gap Analysis
- Consider alternative Frontier Analyses
- Gather more customer language evidence
- Test Belief Shift Journey with target audience
