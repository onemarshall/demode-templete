# Module 03: Glossary

**DeMode™**
_Terminology Specific to Business Commitments_

For master framework terms (Belief, Proof, Result, Vital 3), see `FW_07-Glossary.md`.

---

## Core Concepts

**Business Position**
The strategic business choices defined in FW_07: audience, offer/work, relevant customer value, distinctive commitments, governing belief/stand and the resulting choices and constraints.

M03 develops or reconfirms commitments from authorised M01/M02 sources. M04 and named fact, offer and economic owners retain their authority. The Foundation records the established position; M05 develops how the brand expresses it and makes it recognisable.

Prior positioning may be sound, partial or absent. Use the existing adaptive routes; prior positioning is not an entry requirement.

**Consensus Market Position**
A competitive space defined by shared industry beliefs.

- _Definition:_ Where everyone competes on the same factors because everyone shares the same assumptions.
- _Characteristics:_ Commoditisation, price competition, incremental differentiation.
- _Example:_ The budget airline market where everyone competes on price and route coverage.
- _Opposite:_ Business Commitments

---

**Business Commitments**
Positioning space created by challenging the beliefs that define market boundaries.

- _Definition:_ Differentiated positioning based on rejecting an industry assumption and proving an alternative.
- _Characteristics:_ Fundamental differentiation, belief-based value creation, friction for copycats.
- _Note:_ This is positioning space, not guaranteed market ownership. Must be validated and defended.
- _Test:_ Can you state the belief you're challenging AND how you'll prove your alternative?

---

**Frontier Analysis**
A method for choosing which industry belief boundaries are worth challenging.

- _Definition:_ The direction you choose to push against industry beliefs.
- _Four Analytical Lenses:_
- **Blocked Believers** -- What beliefs prevent people from adopting the new belief?
- **Adjacent Beliefs** -- What beliefs separate this market from adjacent industries?
- **Friction Beliefs** -- What beliefs create friction in the customer journey?
- **Shifting Beliefs** -- What beliefs are becoming obsolete due to trends?
- _Usage:_ Module 03 selects 1–2 priority frontier directions.

---

**Belief Shift Journey (FROM → TO)**
The transformation in customer thinking that your positioning enables.

- _Definition:_ The journey from a limiting belief to an empowering belief that your brand facilitates.
- _Format:_ FROM "[limiting belief]" → TO "[empowering belief]"
- _Example:_ FROM "More features = better software" → TO "Finished software = better software"
- _Usage:_ Anchors messaging, sales, brand identity, and content strategy.
- _Test:_ If you can't state the Belief Shift Journey clearly, the Business Commitments strategy isn't sharp enough.

---

## Strategic Framework Terms

**Conviction Grid**
The four-action strategic framework for belief-based positioning.

- _Actions:_ Question → Reject → Commit → Introduce
- _Rule:_ Every action must have belief rationale, proof commitment, and competitor friction analysis.
- _Principle:_ A grid without proof commitments is a wish list, not a strategy.

| Action        | Definition                                          |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **QUESTION**  | Challenge an industry belief that may be overvalued |
| **REJECT**    | Refuse an industry belief that is false             |
| **COMMIT**    | Stake your conviction and commit to proving it      |
| **INTRODUCE** | Bring a new truth to market                         |

**Grid Columns:**

| Column                  | Definition                             |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Action**              | Question, Reject, Commit, or Introduce |
| **Factor**              | The competitive element being changed  |
| **Belief Challenge**    | What belief justifies this move        |
| **Proof Commitment**    | How we will demonstrate this           |
| **Competitor Friction** | What makes copying costly              |

_Rule:_ Every row must have all columns completed. Empty columns mean incomplete strategy.

---

**Divergence Map**
A visual comparison of your positioning and the industry standard across belief-based factors.

- _Definition:_ A graph showing your level of investment in each factor vs. industry average.
- _Divergence:_ The degree to which your map differs from competitors.
- _Target:_ Divergence should concentrate on factors customers actually notice, care about, and have evidence for in Module 02.

---

**Meaningful Divergence**
The current Module 03 standard for whether your positioning is different in a way that matters.

- _Pass:_ Your divergence concentrates on customer-important factors evidenced in Module 02.
- _Fail:_ Your divergence is spread across low-importance factors, or looks mathematically different without changing the customer's buying decision.
- _Retired term:_ Earlier framework versions used a numeric Divergence Threshold. That heuristic is retired; use Quality Gate 7 instead.

---

**Conviction Anchor**
The single factor that most defines your positioning.

- _Definition:_ The one Conviction Grid move that, if removed, would collapse your differentiation.
- _Test:_ "We are the company that [Conviction Anchor]."
- _Usage:_ Becomes central to messaging and brand identity.

---

## Friction Concepts

**Competitor Friction**
The cost and difficulty of copying your positioning.

- _Definition:_ What would competitors need to change to follow you?
- _Types:_ Belief contradiction (invalidate past claims), operational change (restructure how they work), cultural shift (overcome team resistance), customer explanation (explain reversal to existing customers).
- _Honest limit:_ Friction is not a moat. Competitors CAN copy -- it's just costly.

---

**Friction Analysis**
The honest assessment of what copying your positioning would require.

- _Good:_ "Competitors would need to contradict their core message AND retrain their sales team AND update their pricing model."
- _Bad:_ "Competitors can't copy us because we're unique."
- _Rule:_ Every friction claim must identify specific systemic changes required.

---

**Friction Fantasy**
Overestimating how hard it is for competitors to copy you.

- _Symptom:_ "They can't copy us because [wishful thinking]."
- _Fix:_ Honestly analyse what systemic changes copying would require.
- _Reality:_ With enough resources and motivation, competitors WILL copy successful positioning. Friction buys time, not permanence.

---

## Belief Terms

**Belief-Maintained Canvas**
A map showing competitive factors alongside the beliefs that maintain them.

- _Definition:_ Visual representation of why the market competes on these factors.
- _Format:_ Factor | Artifact Evidence | Maintaining Belief | Consensus Strength
- _Usage:_ Foundation for Conviction Grid analysis.

---

**Blocking Belief**
A specific belief preventing customers or competitors from moving.

- _For Customers:_ The limiting belief keeping them in the Consensus Market Position.
- _For Competitors:_ The belief preventing them from following you.
- _Usage:_ Informs friction analysis and messaging strategy.

---

**The Broken Thing**
The industry belief you're challenging.

- _Definition:_ The consensus assumption that causes customer pain.
- _Source:_ Derived from Industry Default Beliefs (Module 02).
- _Usage:_ Feeds into Brand Identity as the "enemy."
- _Format:_ "[Industry] believes [X], which causes [harm]."

---

**The Hidden Truth**
The insight the market is ignoring.

- _Definition:_ What customers don't see that your belief reveals.
- _Source:_ Derived from Belief Gap (Module 02).
- _Usage:_ Becomes core messaging theme.

---

**Legacy Statement**
The future reality your belief creates.

- _Definition:_ What the world looks like if your belief wins.
- _Source:_ Derived from Divergence Map.
- _Usage:_ The vision that attracts believers.

---

## Proof & Validation Terms

**Proof Match Test**
Alignment between stated belief and actual behaviour.

- _Definition:_ The degree to which your actions match your claims.
- _Test:_ "What does each record demonstrate, who owns it, what can another person inspect, and what sentence does it earn?"
- _Rule:_ Marketing a belief without proving it erodes trust.

---

**Proof Score**
The count of specific proof points supporting your belief.

- _Boundary:_ A count of 3+ is an optional triangulation prompt, not a marketing-permission threshold.
- _Types:_ Product/Feature proof (what you build), Policy/Process proof (how you operate), Story/Track record proof (what you've done).
- _Usage:_ Helps inventory distinct enacted records; readiness depends on claim-matched evidence, rights and the applicable gate.

---

**Proof Deficit Warning**
Having belief claims without demonstration.

- _Symptom:_ Marketing a belief you haven't proven.
- _Result:_ Market smells hypocrisy. Trust erodes.
- _Fix:_ Narrow or withhold the claim, then build evidence of the required type and provenance before marketing it.

**Handoff Pack**
The explicit handoff of locked decisions to downstream modules.

- _Definition:_ Outputs that become inputs for the next module.
- _Rule:_ Once locked, anchors don't change without documented reason.
- _Purpose:_ Prevents context loss between modules and maintains the conviction thread.

## Caution Terms

**Contrarian Trap**
Challenging beliefs simply to be different, without conviction or evidence.

- _Symptom:_ "They believe X, so we'll believe not-X."
- _Fix:_ Ensure genuine conviction + market evidence, not just opposition.
- _Test:_ "Would we hold this belief even if no competitor existed?"

---

**Commercial Viability Gate**
Verification that the Business Commitments strategy represents a real business opportunity.

- _Questions:_ Is the segment big enough? Will they pay? Can we reach them? Does this align with our ability to deliver?
- _Usage:_ Required check before proceeding from Module 03 to Module 04.

## Belief Spectrum Readiness

Market segments categorised by their openness to belief change.

| Segment            | Definition                                                                   |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Belief-Ready**   | Already questioning industry assumptions, actively seeking alternatives      |
| **Belief-Curious** | Sensing something wrong with status quo, but not yet actively seeking change |
| **Belief-Locked**  | Strongly committed to current industry beliefs, resistant to alternatives    |

## Related Master Terms

These terms are defined in `FW_07-Glossary.md`:

- **Belief** (builder input) -- The conviction that authorises all strategy
- **Proof** (builder input) -- The behaviour that demonstrates the belief
- **Result** (market response) -- The belief echoed back by customers
- **Vital 3** -- Belief + Proof produced by the builder; Result returned by the market
- **Conviction Clarity Test** -- Clarity standard for belief statements
- **Artifact** -- Visible evidence that reveals invisible belief
- **Vulnerable Default** -- The subset of Descriptive Defaults that are also normatively challengeable (evidenced customer pain + credible alternative). See `02-Glossary.md`. Module 03 challenges Vulnerable Defaults, not merely Descriptive ones.
