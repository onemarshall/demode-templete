# Module 01: Origin Story

**DeMode™**
_Conviction Discovery & Builder Manifesto_

---

## What This Document Does

This is the output template for Module 01. After the guided conversation (see `01-Prompt.md`), the AI structures the builder's spoken conviction into two deliverables:

- **Output A: Builder Manifesto** — The formal conviction statements that all downstream modules build from
- **Output B: Natural Language Bank** — The builder's authentic vocabulary and energy patterns that feed Module 05 (Brand Identity) and all content generators

**Input:** Guided conversation completed (via `01-Prompt.md`)
**Output:** Builder Manifesto + Natural Language Bank + Belief-First Validation
**Time:** Produced by AI after conversation. Builder reviews and confirms.

---

## The Belief-First Hierarchy

Before you review, understand what you're looking at. The framework runs on three elements: two the builder produces, one the market returns.

| Element | Source | Role | What's Captured Here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Belief** | Builder input | Authorises all strategy | The Core Belief |
| **Proof** | Builder input | Demonstrates the belief | The Non-Negotiable (what you'll prove) |
| **Result** | Market response | The belief echoed back by customers | The Legacy (what success looks like) |

Belief without Proof is empty -- and worse, claiming belief without demonstrating it reads as betrayal once customers test it. Proof without Belief is generic. Result confirms both; its absence signals one of the first two is missing. The sequence is directional: you can't start with Result and work backwards.

## Quality Gate

**This output passes when:**

- [ ] **Specificity:** The Broken Thing names a belief competitors hold, not just a generic problem
- [ ] **Differentiation:** Competitors would disagree with the Hidden Truth
- [ ] **Cost:** The Non-Negotiable involves real sacrifice
- [ ] **Proof Path:** There's a clear way to demonstrate (not just claim) the belief
- [ ] **Prospective Constraint Test:** Before inspecting current artefacts, does the manifesto require a future identity-bearing choice and rule out a plausible alternative?
- [ ] **Natural Language Captured:** Output B contains authentic vocabulary distinct from Output A's formal language

**If all met:** Proceed to the Evidence Matrix (`01_03-Evidence-Matrix.md`)
**If any missing:** Revise the relevant section before proceeding
**If you cannot articulate a Core Belief:** This isn't failure — it's information. You may need more time, more experience, or more honest reflection. Consider whether this venture is ready for strategy work, or whether you need to return to the Preparation Guide.
