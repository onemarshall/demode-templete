# Quality Gates — Digital Direction

## Strategy-to-action starter plans

Apply `FW_41`. A relevant accepted strategy's output set includes its recommended source-derived Starter Plan, exact opening and eleven sections, current hash/decision/consequence/evidence trace, separate recommendation class and human lifecycle, supported roles/dependencies/assets, conditional campaign requirements and specialist handoff. Reject generic tasks, invented budget/capacity/owners/calendar precision, missing qualifications or strategy changes hidden in planning. A required recommendation class does not mean the plan has been adopted. Record non-applicability rather than filling an irrelevant section. Existing gates and release permissions remain in force; local structural passes do not prove specialist usefulness or client comprehension.

> **Client-language translation gate:** Apply `FW_27` before passing any client-facing output. Preserve the digital decisions but translate framework labels into plain business language; fail if a capable client cannot state what has been decided, why, what it changes and what would make it return.

## Digital Direction operating gates — required

Pass the following gates in order. These are M09's primary operating gates; earlier supporting checks still apply where they do not conflict.

1. **Authority:** current M04 RouteAuthorization, usable M05–M08 directions, claims boundary and current business/offer state are present. Conditional or stale inputs permit only an honestly labelled candidate where they do not materially determine the conclusion; otherwise block lock.
2. **Ecosystem:** assess the relevant owned and external estate before architecture, including the existing-state decision where applicable.
3. **Digital jobs:** derive jobs from the business and authorised route; do not use a generic website or funnel inventory.
4. **Visitor decisions:** identify the decision and material uncertainty associated with every material job.
5. **Candidate architecture:** generate candidates from decisions, not page-count or template convention.
6. **Separate-Destination Test:** every approved permanent destination passes distinct decision, material uncertainty, separation value, merge loss and maintenance justification.
7. **Operating gates:** classify capacity, maintenance ownership, accessibility, privacy/data, content readiness and production competence separately for each affected destination/capability.
8. **Architecture comparison:** compare the smallest credible option with the strongest credible expansion whenever a material alternative exists.
9. **Conservative default:** when evidence is inconclusive, the smaller maintainable option wins.
10. **Human decision:** a named authorised M09 owner accepts the recommendation and comparison record before architecture lock.
11. **Page briefs:** every approved permanent destination has the full strategic brief: job, visitor state/decision, uncertainty, inherited sources, minimum information/proof, claims boundary, next routes and exits, exclusions, owners, review and merge condition.
12. **Claims/proof integrity:** controlled method proof remains distinct from client-outcome proof; anticipated buyer questions never become asserted buyer prevalence.
13. **Adaptation authority:** Levels 1–3 and their return conditions are explicit.

Where possible, apply a failed gate to the destination or capability it affects. Do not silently invalidate separate parts of the direction that remain safe.

## FW_32 accountability gate

For every material accepted decision, require an exact decision ID, complete Implementation Consequences, non-monetary Cost Drivers and a Decision Measurement Contract with intended effect, implementation proof, baseline requirement, evidence, attribution ceiling, evidence owner, review decisions, stop condition and narrowest return point. Fail a monetary estimate or `derivable` driver when architecture, scope or production standard is unresolved; fail any collapse of public site, authenticated workspace or future Self-Service.

## Editorial Readability Gate

Apply `FW_27`. Pass only when the site's commercial job and buyer decision path precede architecture jargon; architecture, copy, design, build, launch and live validation stay separate; and every technical term is needed by the intended reader. Record structural evidence; keep cold-reader comprehension `pending`.

Fail mandatory long-section treatment of candidate page sequences, a Home page carrying the complete system, unclear Direct Consulting, Evidence pages that expose internal machinery, an impersonal or excessive Contact route, or any merge of publishing and the authenticated client workspace. Page density during copy production remains a live validation risk rather than a reason to redesign the accepted architecture now.

## Runtime Architecture Boundary Gate

Pass only when governing architecture uses the current approved RouteAuthorization and accepted upstream strategy. A bounded response contains only the authorised minimum public architecture and page purposes; added copy, keywords, handoff material, offers, routes or locations fail.

Missing, stale or insufficient route authority blocks governing architecture and copy and returns to the exact Module 04 or human-owned route decision. Fail a local substitute approval. Conflicting extensions remain outside the accepted lock until that decision is recorded; creating, scheduling or silently adding their pages fails.

## Strategy Multi-View Gate

For each routed Digital Direction view, apply `FW_26`: verify standard metadata and state inheritance; preserve the minimum route, buyer journey, architecture, fit and proof requirements, valid non-conversion, release boundary, trade-off and accepted decision; require the Executive View to support leadership understanding without a second approval request; require a 7–10-slide narrative with speaker notes and source trace; reject unauthorised features, targets or technical commitments.

Verify the website journey and copy preserve every fixed business-definition field in the approved Business Foundation, remain compatible with its Business Manifesto and express the canonical Module 05 strategy without redefining the business. The site must use the worldview across visitor decisions rather than paste or recreate the manifesto. A missing, stale or mismatched Business Foundation blocks the module and returns to the owning source.

## Consultancy Judgement Gate

Pass only when the first page states the digital-investment recommendation; evidence and confidence are explicit; a credible alternative and accepted trade-off are shown; the client decision, conditions, owner, progression and invalidation are clear; and the complete Consultancy Decision Record has been checked transiently with its final state recorded compactly in `RUN-MANIFEST.md`. ## How to Use Quality Gates

## Client Output Gate

Before lock, verify the required Digital Direction order and `09_00`–`09_07` anchors exist; the governing recommendation and four decision states are clear; ecosystem and jobs precede architecture; every page/destination brief is usable; and architecture, copy, design, build, QA and live state remain separate. When delivery follows, `09-digital-strategy-practitioner-brief.md` must use Practitioner Brief V3 (`view-version: 3.0.0`, `format: Langfell-hybrid-practitioner-brief`), preserve the common spine, include the Digital-Direction-specific middle and pass its existing gates. Internal controls in the client body fail.

1. **Complete the module** -- Run the module prompt, generate outputs
2. **Run the Quality Gate** -- Check all items for that gate
3. **If all pass** -- Proceed to next module
4. **If any fail** -- Revise outputs before proceeding

**Complete every gate.** Resolving generic outputs now prevents downstream rework.

## Gate 0: Governance Check (Before Starting)

Four upstream modules are required, with different gate conditions. Module 08 is a hard gate: without content, there is no site to architect. Modules 05, 06 and 07 each govern a specific part of the result. See `09-README.md` Gate 0 for the full four-part specification.

**Required — Hard Gate (Module 08):**

| Check              | Pass                                                  | Fail                                           |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Module 08 complete | Content pillars, proof hierarchy, objection map built | Any sub-element missing — no site to architect |

**Required — Dimensional Gates (Modules 04, 05, 06, 07):** Failure in each damages one specific dimension of the result.

| Check | Pass | Failure Mode if Missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Module 04 RouteAuthorization | `status` is `Approved`/`Overridden`; `generation_permission` matches launch intent; surface/page scope within `permitted_*` | Site over-scoped or published without approved route |
| Module 05 complete | Brand Strategy, Belief Shift Journey, Voice, Copywriting rules, Claims Boundaries, Filter | Site character reads generic — no conviction expressed |
| Values can be listed | 3–5 core values with meanings and voice rules | Voice choices default to convention |
| Module 06 complete | Belief-ready segments, 10 marketing statements | Above-the-fold messaging defaults to category language |
| Module 07 complete | Objection responses, sales narrative, qualification language | Conversion paths hit decision moments with no support |

**If Module 08 fails (hard gate):** Cannot proceed. Return to Module 08.
**If Module 04, 05, 06, or 07 fails (dimensional gate):** Stop. Return to the failing module. Each failure is visible to the market in its specific dimension.

**Three-Way Alignment Test:**

**If any criterion fails:** Keyword serves partial purpose only. Document the trade-off before proceeding.

## Final Module Gate: Structural Coherence Test

A cold reader must be able to answer these questions:

- Is this a recommendation or the final website?
- Why this page count, and is it permanent?
- What decision does each page support?
- Are the candidate sections mandatory?
- How are overload, thinness, merging and splitting assessed?
- When may a page or destination be added?
- What remains to validate, and what does acceptance authorise?
- Were website resources and ongoing publication assessed separately?
- Were contextual answers and FAQ/Q&A assessed?
- Were embedded evidence, a single Evidence page, cases and a library assessed against proof maturity?
- Does every destination decision state its need, authority, maintenance requirements and reason?

Any ambiguous answer fails the relevant section; do not repair it with a general disclaimer.

Module 09 delivers **structural coherence**: architecture, page briefs and copy that express the upstream decisions clearly enough to guide Module 10’s visual and interaction work.

**Run this test before handoff to Module 10:**

> "If you read only the section order and headings of every page — no copy, no visuals — would you understand the brand's strategic priorities: what it leads with, what it defers, what it omits?"

- [ ] Yes — Structural coherence is working. The content structure is ready for Module 10.

**If no:** Section ordering or page selection contradicts upstream commitments. Fix before handoff.

## Fail Conditions

### Automatic Fail

| Condition                                              | Result                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Page briefs not human-confirmed before content         | FAIL -- Get confirmation                     |
| Copy contains "[Placeholder]" or generic text          | FAIL -- Write actual content                 |
| Voice inconsistent across pages                        | FAIL -- Review against Module 05 Voice + Copywriting rules |
| Claims exceed Evidence Matrix boundaries               | FAIL -- Review and qualify                   |
| Keywords drive traffic but not belief-aligned visitors | FAIL -- Revise keyword strategy              |
| Structure doesn't support belief journey               | FAIL -- Revise site architecture             |

### Recovery

| Failure               | Recovery                                       |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| No human confirmation | Pause, get explicit sign-off before proceeding |
| Placeholder content   | Complete all sections with actual copy         |
| Voice drift           | Review all pages against Module 05 Voice + Copywriting rules |
| Overclaiming          | Cut or qualify claims that exceed boundaries   |
| Generic keywords      | Find terms that express specific belief        |
| Structure issues      | Return to 09-01 and revise architecture        |
