## Governing derivation sequence — required

M09 is **Digital Direction**. It decides what digital must do before deciding how many permanent destinations are required. Its complete output may be described as **Digital Direction and Minimum Digital Architecture**. The retained folder name and technical identifiers preserve runtime compatibility. They do not define the client-facing method.

Run this chain in order. Do not reverse it:

```text
current authority and operating reality
→ digital role, ecosystem and jobs
→ visitor/customer decisions and progressive depth
→ candidate permanent destinations
→ separate-destination tests and operating gates
→ smallest credible architecture versus strongest credible expansion
→ named human decision
→ strategic page briefs and production handoff
```

A page count is an output, never an input. Do not infer a conventional website, consulting journey, marketing site, blog, ecommerce flow, product onboarding or a fixed number of pages. The primary environment may be an application, booking flow, member portal, marketplace, partner platform, product interface, documentation system, ecommerce estate or another digital environment. Derive the estate type from the authorities and business state.

### Authority and starting-state check

Inspect the current M04 `RouteAuthorization`; usable M05 Brand Strategy; usable M06 Marketing Strategy; usable M07 Sales Strategy; usable M08 Content Strategy; current claims boundary; and current business/offer state. Classify the starting state as new business, existing business, rebrand, repositioning, redesign, migration, business-model transition, product launch, sub-brand, mature estate or another named state. For an existing estate, classify each relevant component: retain, strengthen, revise, migrate, consolidate, retire, replace or investigate.

An exploratory candidate can be useful where authority is conditional, stale or unresolved. A locked architecture cannot be produced where that defect materially changes the recommendation. State the affected authority and keep `production_authority_state` blocked; never silently promote a candidate into current authority.

### Digital jobs, decisions and progressive depth

Derive only the jobs that this business and route require (for example recognition, explanation, comparison, proof, trust, fit, purchase, booking, onboarding, support, retention, usage, community, discovery, education or partner enablement). For every material job, name the decision a person must be able to make and the uncertainty it resolves. Determine how much depth the journey needs: recognition, distinction, point of view, mechanism, detailed method/proof and commercial decision. These levels need not become separate pages.

### Candidate destinations and the complexity burden

Generate candidates before approval. Record working name, digital job, visitor decision, material uncertainty, source authority, proof requirement, next routes, content readiness and maintenance requirement.

Each proposed permanent destination must pass all five tests:

1. **Distinct visitor decision** — what decision does it support?
2. **Distinct material uncertainty** — what important uncertainty does it resolve?
3. **Separation value** — why is separation better than carrying that job elsewhere?
4. **Merge loss** — what clarity, proof, progression or usability is materially lost if merged?
5. **Maintenance justification** — does the benefit justify its ongoing burden?

If it fails, merge, defer or reject it. For every provisional destination, record what would trigger a merge and what would confirm the need for separation. A permanent owned-thinking destination additionally requires a distinct job, approved sufficient content, a maintenance owner and route relevance. Architecture readiness without launch is valid.

### Discrete operating gates

Assess each affected destination or capability against these separate conditions:

- **Capacity:** can the business operate it?
- **Maintenance ownership:** who keeps it accurate?
- **Accessibility:** can it meet the applicable baseline?
- **Privacy/data:** are forms, tracking, accounts, personal data, cookies and obligations governable?
- **Content readiness:** is approved information and evidence sufficient for its job?
- **Production competence:** is there a credible responsible implementation route?

A failed gate blocks or constrains the affected destination where possible; it does not automatically invalidate all Digital Direction.

### Architecture comparison and decision protocol

Compare the smallest credible architecture with the strongest materially credible expanded architecture whenever there is a meaningful alternative. Compare visitor clarity, progressive disclosure, proof legibility, perceived complexity, navigation burden, content readiness, maintenance burden, marketing usefulness, sales usefulness, specialist-handoff quality and route fit. Record candidates, criteria, evidence, unresolved uncertainty, recommendation, named human owner, human decision and rationale. Professional judgement may make an initial recommendation; label its evidence state honestly.

When evidence is inconclusive, select the smaller maintainable architecture. The additional destination carries the burden of demonstrating material added value; the smaller architecture does not have to prove that merging is harmless.

### Adaptation authority

- **Level 1 — local adaptation:** wording, section order, imagery, layout, accessibility improvement, implementation technology or minor interaction may change without an M09 rerun only when visitor decision, route branch, proof state, claims exposure and maintenance commitment are unchanged.
- **Level 2 — M09 review:** required for a new permanent destination, removal of a core destination, persistent form, major navigation restructure, permanent knowledge area, proof destination or materially changed journey. A navigation change is material if it changes a primary decision, route branch, proof/claims exposure or permanent maintenance commitment.
- **Level 3 — upstream return:** return to the owning source when the proposal changes buyer, Brand, offer, positioning, Marketing route, Sales architecture, Proof/Content strategy, commercial model or route authority.

Campaign landing pages, event destinations and account-specific temporary surfaces inherit Digital Direction, claims boundaries and relevant decisions. They are not permanent architecture unless M09 reviews and promotes them. A future self-service product route, if separately authorised, requires a full rerun; do not describe the current business as SaaS.

## Architecture Extension, Revision and Validation — Required

A new public page is justified only when it serves a distinct audience state or visitor decision; cannot be handled clearly in an existing page; has enough approved content and evidence; has a clear journey role; has an owner and maintenance model; stays inside offer and claims authority; does not duplicate publishing or workspace roles; improves decision quality; and cannot be handled better as a contextual answer, article, publication, evidence record or case study.

Do not add a page to make the site appear larger, give every framework term a destination, publish an unavailable product, duplicate a category, compensate for unclear copy, create navigation symmetry, store ownerless content, hide essential commercial information, mirror another platform, anticipate an unobserved FAQ or create an empty proof library.

Use this revision rule verbatim:

> The architecture should change only when the reason for the change is recorded. A page may be added, merged, divided, renamed or removed because of clearer strategy, approved offer change, stronger evidence, repeated comprehension failure, observed visitor behaviour, search evidence or implementation constraints. It should not drift through isolated copy, design or technology decisions.

Every change record names the current route, observed problem, supporting evidence, visitor decision affected, proposed change, expected clarity, new maintenance or complexity and approver.

At Module 09 stage, “working” means the architecture is traceable to approved audience, offer, belief, sales and evidence decisions; every page owns a distinct visitor decision; no important buyer question lacks a home; unavailable products are not being sold; and copy and design can proceed without inventing strategy. It does not mean conversion, ranking or commercial performance has been proven. After implementation, test first-read comprehension, accurate description, route behaviour, fit-conversation quality, objections, enquiry relevance, abandonment, accessibility, search and referral language. Do not invent fixed conversion thresholds before a baseline exists.

Acceptance authorises the governing baseline, page-copy briefs, content and design testing within approved page jobs and later evidence-led revision. It does not authorise mandatory treatment of every candidate section, equal page lengths, a permanent page count, finished copy, design, technology, build, migration, analytics conclusions, search or conversion claims, or launch.

**Full Run completeness:** the client document must retain the ecosystem assessment; reasoned destination decisions; smallest defensible architecture; one decision-led brief for every page; search and content-destination strategy; coherence and accessibility review; extension and revision rules; validation and launch states; index; and authorisation boundary. A page list without visitor decision, uncertainty, ecosystem relationship, minimum proof, merge/split triggers and validation is incomplete.

## Critique Loop

Module 09 produces customer-facing web copy and front-door content. After generating each page brief or content section, run a **Statement Self-Critique** or route to a human reviewer. No `critique-agent.md` exists; do not invoke a non-existent agent.

**Critique lens:** Accessibility — Will the target audience (founders of founder-led companies, growing organisations, or established enterprises seeking a coherent, evidence-derived strategy) understand this immediately? Is jargon contextualised? Is the copy plain and direct?

**Flow:**
1. Generate page brief/content → Self-critique
2. If it passes → Move to next page/section
3. If it fails → Revise → Re-critique (up to 3 rounds)
4. Repeat for all page briefs and content sections
5. If deadlock after 3 rounds → Human review event; record reviewer, decision, and rationale

### 09_02 Page Briefs

**Purpose:** Produce one decision-led Page Brief per page: page job, visitor decision, uncertainty, ecosystem relationship, provisional sequence, proof, merge/split triggers and validation, all derived from upstream Handoff Packs.

### System Prompt — Two-Step Translation Process (per FW_16)

Page Briefs are the deliverable most vulnerable to silent drift. A brief can read fluently while its sections were chosen by analogy to other websites rather than from the upstream belief and conversation architecture. The two-step process forces each Brief to anchor on a specific, named taxonomy of upstream elements before any section is sketched.

**Per-page anchoring taxonomy (the working answer to "what does 'per page' mean for the Translation Discipline").** Every Page Brief must declare four anchoring coordinates before any section is written:

| Coordinate | Source | What it governs |
| ---------- | ------ | --------------- |
| **(a) Belief Stage** | Module 06 Belief Stage Mapping — the specific stage this page targets | Audience state at page entry; FROM → TO language; what the page is trying to shift |
| **(b) Movement** | Module 07 Five Movements — the movement this page corresponds to (if conversion-path-relevant; may be "n/a" for non-invitation pages like proof libraries) | Conversion behaviour the page is meant to produce; objection-handling load |
| **(c) Content Pillar(s)** | Module 08 Content Pillars — which pillar(s) this page expresses | Subject matter; proof assets the page draws from |
| **(d) Brand Identity elements** | Module 05 Voice + Copywriting rules + Claims Boundaries + Filter + relevant Expression Elements | Voice, writing rules, claim limits, exclusions, Module 10 notes |
