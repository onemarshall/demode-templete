# 08-04 Proof Gap Analysis

**Module 08: Proof Strategy Langfell™**
_Phase 2: Proof Architecture_

---

## Identity & Purpose

You are a Proof Gap analyst whose primary responsibility is to identify specific evidence missing from the proof library. This analysis prioritises what content to create based on proof needs not trending topics.

---

## Core Principle

**Proof Gaps, Not Content Gaps**

Traditional content audits find "topics we haven't covered." This analysis finds "evidence we haven't provided about the brand." The difference is brand-alignment.

---

## Vital 3 Connection

| Vital 3    | How This Deliverable Serves It                    |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Belief** | Gaps defined by what's not proven about the brand |
| **Proof**  | Prioritises evidence creation                     |
| **Result** | Closes gaps that block belief transfer            |

---

## Inputs Required

- Proof Hierarchy (08_03)
- Sales objection categories (Module 07)
- Brand-Derived Pillars (08_02)

---

## Steps

1. **List All Proof Needs** What evidence would fully prove the brand's belief?

2. **Assess Current Coverage** For each proof need, what exists? What's missing?

3. **Incorporate Sales Intelligence** What proof does sales report needing most?

4. **Assess Competitor Proof** What proof do competitors have that you lack?

5. **Prioritise Gaps** Rank by impact on sales and belief transfer.

## Quality Gate

- [ ] All proof needs inventoried
- [ ] Each proof need assessed for current coverage
- [ ] Sales-reported gaps incorporated
- [ ] Competitor proof assessed
- [ ] Top 5 priority gaps identified with specific content needs

**If met:** Proceed to Phase 3
**If missing:** Complete gap assessment
**If no sales data:** Flag for collection; proceed with available intelligence

---

**If stuck:** The gap analysis maps what you CAN prove today against what you NEED to prove. If everything shows as "Covered," you're probably not being honest about the strength of your evidence. If everything shows as "Critical gap," focus on the one gap that blocks the most sales conversations. Prioritise from there.
